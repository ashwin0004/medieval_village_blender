# Environment Design: Medieval Fantasy Alpine Village

## Visual Identity & World-Building
- **Setting**: A secluded medieval settlement in a sub-alpine river glade, nestled beneath towering pine-clad peaks and misty mountain crags.
- **Architectural Style**: Blend of High Medieval half-timbering (*Fachwerk*) with Romanesque stone masonry (church and defensive watchtower).
- **Time Periods Represented**: Built over centuries—weathered stone foundations with younger timber-framed expansions.

## Composition & Landmark Focal Points
1. **The River Glade & Stone Bridge**:
   - The central natural axis dividing the quiet woodland and the bustling village heart.
   - Flowing water with transparent shallows, submerged boulders, and foaming cascades.
2. **The Watchtower**:
   - Dominating the northern hill, guarding the river approach and alpine pass.
   - Built with massive coursed masonry, arched viewing arrowslits, and wooden hoardings.
3. **The Marketplace & Tavern Square**:
   - The communal hub filled with wooden market stalls, produce baskets, stacked ale barrels, iron hanging signs, and merchant carts.
4. **Forest Margins**:
   - Smooth transition from manicured village pathways into wild dense pine and oak woods.
   - Understory populated with mossy rocks, wild bushes, ferns, and wildflowers.

## Lighting & Color Scripts
- **Daylight**: Clean natural daylight (5500K) with crisp cast shadows beneath timber eaves and glistening water specular highlights.
- **Sunset**: Rich golden-hour lighting (3200K) skimming mountain ridges, casting warm amber highlights on stone walls and water ripples.
- **Nighttime**: Deep indigo moonlight (7500K) balanced by warm orange lantern fire (2200K) spilling from windows and market posts.
